# Photo-pack people who are NOT on the 2027 candidate list

The research pack has 30 portraits. 8 belong to certified 2027 candidates
(matched by hand in `scripts/build-photo-pack-index.mjs`); the remaining
**22 are not on the ticket** and appear in no profile today. They are
serving governors, senators, ministers and party figures.

They have no verified party/office/state in this dataset, so any profile
built from this list uses **sample placeholder data** until each field is
sourced. Photos are rendered at the owner's instruction and are credited
"Licence not verified" where the rights were never confirmed.

## Notes
- `13 Cleopas Zuwoghe` (NDC national chairman) has **no image**: the saved file
  is a Bing results page, not a photograph. Still needs sourcing.
- `19 Tajudeen Abbas` is the only low-resolution hold-out (132x132); its
  original source URL is now dead.
- `22 Mai Mala Buni` was replaced with a Wikimedia Commons portrait
  (600x900, **CC BY-SA 4.0**, author Fula boy) - higher resolution and now
  correctly cleared.
- `28 Umo Eno` was replaced with the official Akwa Ibom State Government
  portrait (1484x1878); reuse terms still not independently verified.
- `30 Barau Jibrin` carries a confirmed open licence (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0).
- `24 Abba Kabir Yusuf` (Kano State governor) is deliberately **not** matched
  to APP's "Kabiru Yusuf" on the ticket: similar name, different person.
