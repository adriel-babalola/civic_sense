1.BOLA AHMED TINUBU
Political Role : 
President, Federal Republic of Nigeria, APC presidential candidate for 2027, former Governor of Lagos State
Political Record And Impact: 
President Tinubu removed fuel subsidy, unified forex rates,launched student loan schemes for 300k+ students, boosted federal revenue, and started major projects like Lagos-Calabar Highway and CND initiatives
Questionable Political Record And Blunders 
Immediate petrol-subsidy removal was followed by sharp increase in fuel and transport costs. Foreign-exchange reforms were accompanied by substancial naira depreciation and inflationary pressure. Retaining the Musslim-Muslim ticket with Kashim Shetima for 2027 has kept religious balancing controversy active. Critics have also questioned how quickly households have felt the benefits of subsidy savings

President Tinubu was involved in a 1993 U.S. asset-forfeiture proceeding involving about $460,000. It was a civil forfeiture proceeding, not a criminal conviction . Others include ;
